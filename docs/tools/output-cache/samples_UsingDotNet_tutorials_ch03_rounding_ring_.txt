1. Round of x.5 with the two modes:
   value   ToEven   AwayFromZero
   0.50    0        1
   1.50    2        2
   2.50    2        3
   3.50    4        4
   -0.50   0        -1
   -1.50   -2       -2
   -2.50   -2       -3

2. Round with digits:
   Round(3.14159, 2)             = 3.140
   Round(0.125, 2): ToEven 0.120, AwayFromZero 0.130
   Round(0.135, 2): ToEven 0.140, AwayFromZero 0.140

3. Floor, Ceiling, Truncate:
   value   Floor  Ceiling  Truncate
   2.70    2      3        2
   2.20    2      3        2
   -2.20   -3     -2       -2
   -2.70   -3     -2       -2
   Floor goes down, Ceiling goes up, Truncate cuts the decimals (toward zero).

4. division with a remainder:
   17 / 5 = 3 remainder 2
   -17 / 5 = -3 remainder -2  (the remainder has the sign of -17)

5. Clamp, Sign, Abs:
   Clamp(-5  , 0, 100) = 0
   Clamp(40  , 0, 100) = 40
   Clamp(250 , 0, 100) = 100
   Sign(-8) = -1, Sign(0) = 0, Sign(8) = 1, Abs(-8) = 8

6. powers and logarithms:
   Pow(2, 10) = 1024, Sqrt(144) = 12, Cbrt(27) = 3
   Log10(1000) = 3, Log2(1024) = 10, Log(E) = 1

7. ten items of 0.125 each (one eighth of a unit):
   rounded to cents with ToEven:       1.20   (the exact total is 1.25)
   rounded to cents with AwayFromZero: 1.30
   A shop uses ONE rule everywhere and rounds only the final amount, or the amount of each line, as the law of the country says.
